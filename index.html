FAQ

Straight answers, including the limits.

Where does the data come from?

Public building-permit records, pulled nightly from the City of Sacramento, Placer County, and City of Stockton permit portals. Every listing shows its permit number, so you can pull the source record yourself and check us.

How fresh is it, really?

A permit filed Monday is in Tuesday morning's digest. These are application/file dates — you see the job when it's filed, before review and issuance, which is when it matters for getting in early. Compare: the city's open-data file trails the portal by about two to three weeks.

What exactly is in each listing?

Address, permit number, jurisdiction, scope (reroof, change-out, PV + storage…), status, and the permit's work description. One honest limit: the portals' public exports don't include contractor names or job values, and we don't dress the feed up with data we don't have.

Do you sell homeowner phone numbers or emails?

No. This is permit intelligence — what's being built, where, and what's involved. No homeowner contact lists, no call files. Reaching the homeowner is your craft; knowing the job exists before your competitors do is ours.

Which trades and areas?

Roofing, solar, and HVAC feeds. Coverage is the City of Sacramento, Placer County, and the City of Stockton, with more metro jurisdictions on the roadmap. Filter any feed by ZIP.

How does the trial work?

Fourteen days of the real daily digest, free — no card required to start. Near the end of the trial we'll email you a link to add a card and keep it going at $49/mo. If you don't add one, the feed simply pauses. You're never charged for a trial, and there's no retention department to call — cancel anytime in two clicks.

Why not just check the permit portal myself?

You can — it's public. It'll take you an evening a week across three portals that don't search by date or trade, or about $49 a month not to. Contractors who try us usually did the portal grind first.
